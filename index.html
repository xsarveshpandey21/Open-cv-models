Processing Pipeline

  • 1
    MTCNN Landmark Detection

    Locates facial structures and extracts precise mouth corner landmarks (Left & Right mouth corners).

  • 2
    ROI & Shi-Tomasi Feature Seeding

    Computes centered lip bounding box and seeds up to 25 corner tracking points inside the lip region.

  • 3
    Lucas-Kanade Optical Flow

    Tracks corner points frame-to-frame between periodic MTCNN re-detection cycles for smooth tracking.

  • 4
    Shutil Directory Archiving

    Segments video into 5-second raw and cropped lip video chunks, auto-generating ZIP archives and CSV logs.
