Tracking Architecture

  • 1
    Live Webcam / Video Input

    Real-time OpenCV capture from native webcam (index 0) or arbitrary video formats (MP4, AVI, MOV) with auto-resolution detection.

  • 2
    Face, Eyes & Nose Landmark Detection

    MTCNN extracts primary face bounding box (x, y, w, h), left/right eye pupils, and nose tip coordinates.

  • 3
    Left & Right Ear Cranial Geometry

    Calculates exact ear tragus coordinates and bounding boxes from cranial lateral vectors, computing real-time ear span distance.

  • 4
    Shi-Tomasi & Lucas-Kanade Optical Flow

    Seeds corner tracking points in lip ROI and propagates motion delta vectors across all facial landmarks at 30+ FPS.

  • 5
    Cyber HUD Overlay & CSV Telemetry

    Renders real-time landmark coordinate badges, cyber HUD panel, and exports full spatial coordinates to structured CSV.

Core Feature Breakdown

Live Webcam Studio

Instant camera 0 live tracking with live preview window, hotkeys (ESC to exit, H for HUD, B for boxes), and real-time FPS.

Whole Face Bounding Box

Displays full face bounding box (x, y, w, h) and face center coordinates with high-tech corner accents.

Left & Right Ear Tracking

Calculates and tracks Left Ear & Right Ear landmarks, bounding boxes, and real-time ear span measurement (px).

Dual Eyes & Inter-Eye Distance

Tracks Left and Right Eye centers, eye ROI bounding boxes, and dynamic inter-eye distance metric in pixels.

Nose Landmark Anchor

Tracks nose tip keypoint and ROI box, providing the central anchor for facial geometry and orientation.

Lips & Optical Flow

Tracks mouth corners, lip center, Shi-Tomasi feature points, motion trails, and fitted parabolic curves.

Quick Start & Live Webcam Command

# 1. Run Live Webcam Tracking directly (Camera 0)
python track_features.py --live

# 2. Run with Recorded Video file
python track_features.py --input tracked_lips.mp4 --show

# 3. Launch Interactive Streamlit Web Studio
streamlit run app.py
